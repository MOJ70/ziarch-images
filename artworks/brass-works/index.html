Artworks / Brass Works

Brass Works

The brass works are made by corrosion: brass plates treated with chemical solutions oxidise into patinas whose colours and textures draw their own patterns. The studies began as material swatches for the material suite of the ARC 604 studio and grew into a series in which the reaction itself becomes the image.
